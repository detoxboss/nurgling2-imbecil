## New upstream architecture — adopted as-is

The gathering-bot rework brings a consistent shape across `SmelterAction`, `LeatherAction`,
`DFrameFishAction`, and `DFrameHidesAction`: sum free capacity across every not-yet-full container in
the work area, issue one bulk `TakeItems2.takeAny(...)` call per round (sized against the observed item
shape via `fillTargets` where the container is irregular), distribute, and terminate on a round that
places nothing. Supporting pieces: `Container`'s typed capacity attributes (`Space`/`Tetris`/
`ItemCount`), stack-depth-aware sizing in `DepositItemsToSpecArea` (free *cells* converted to free
*items* via `StackSupporter`), gob re-resolution before every stockpile/container visit
(`TakeItems2.takeFromPile`, `FindNISBox`'s tracked-gob constructor) to survive a stockpile being
destroyed mid-drain, and a progress-measured, stall-bounded wait in `WaitItemFromPile`. None of this
required fork modification — the fork carried no local edits to any of these files. Recorded in full,
with the migration path onto this infrastructure for `FreeContainersInUnboxZone`, in
`docs/fork-maintenance-backlog.md`.
